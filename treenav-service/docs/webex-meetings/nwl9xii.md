# Enable Webex Assistant for Webex Meetings

- [Control Hub](#id%5F132145)
- [Site Administration](#id%5F132171)

Webex Assistant is enabled by default. As a Webex administrator, change the settings or verify that you enabled Webex Assistant for Meetings in Control Hub.

Before you begin

Webex Assistant is available in Meetings and Webex Webinars. This feature requires Cisco Webex video platform version 2.0 with joining meetings from video systems enabled.

| 1 | Sign in to [Control Hub](https://admin.webex.com), and go to Services \>  Meeting.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| - | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 2 | Click Sites, choose the Webex site that you want to change the settings for, and then click Settings.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| 3 | Under Common Settings, select Site Options.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| 4 | Find Webex Assistantand configure it as follows: Ensure Enable Webex Assistantis checked to make Webex Assistant available in meetings and webinars on your site. Check Enable real-time translation to allow users to [show real-time meeting translations in their own language](https://help.webex.com/nqzpeei/) during their meeting or webinar. Real-time translation is a paid add-on for Webex Assistant for Webex Meetings. This option is only visible for those who've paid for the add-on. To add support to Webex Assistant, contact your Webex account team. Uncheck Turn on the audio announcement (" Webex Assistant  is enabled to save audio highlights") to disable the audio announcement when you turn on Webex Assistant in your meeting or webinar. |
| 5 | Click Save.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |

Real-time translation is a paid add-on for Webex Assistant for Webex Meetings. This option is only visible for those who've paid for the add-on.
