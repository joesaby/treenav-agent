# Sign in to a shared phone (Extension Mobility)

## Sign yourself in to a shared phone

If extension mobility is configured, you can sign in to a different phone in your network and have it act the same as your phone. When you log in to an IP phone using the extension mobility feature, the phone retrieves your personal settings such as speed dials, phone directory, call history, and voicemail settings.

Before you begin

Your administrator has configured extension mobility.

| 1 | Press ![the Settings hard key](https://cisco-api.ingeniuxondemand.com/DITA/content/en/us/td/i/400001-500000/470001-480000/478001-479000/478218.png).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| - | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| 2 | To access EM service, do one of the following actions: ![For 9811, 9841,9851, and 9861](https://cisco-api.ingeniuxondemand.com/DITA/content/en/us/td/i/400001-500000/480001-490000/489001-490000/489180.png) Select Network and service \> Custom services \> EM ![9871 tab](https://cisco-api.ingeniuxondemand.com/DITA/content/en/us/td/i/400001-500000/480001-490000/483001-484000/483152.png) Select Custom services \> EM |
| 3 | Enter UserID and PIN that you administrator has created for you.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| 4 | Click Submit. You see Login Successful message.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |

## Sign out from the shared phone

After you complete your work, you must sign out of the shared phone. 

| 1 | Press ![the Settings hard key](https://cisco-api.ingeniuxondemand.com/DITA/content/en/us/td/i/400001-500000/470001-480000/478001-479000/478218.png). |
| - | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 2 | Select Network and settings.                                                                                                                         |
| 3 | Select Custom services \> EM.                                                                                                                        |
| 4 | Click Yes to sign out the phone.                                                                                                                     |
