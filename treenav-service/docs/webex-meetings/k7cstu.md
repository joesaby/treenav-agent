# Webex App | Join a meeting

| Method                              | Details                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| ----------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Join from your meetings list        | Join the meeting from the green Join button that appears in your meeting list 5 minutes before the meeting's scheduled start time. See [Webex App \| Join a meeting from your meetings list](https://help.webex.com/nmegiuz/)                                |
| Join by entering the meeting number | If you know the meeting number, enter it in the app to join the meeting. See [Webex App \| Join a meeting with a meeting number or video address](https://help.webex.com/nx9i2ccb/)                                                                          |
| Join someone's Personal Room        | If someone asks you to join their [Personal Room](https://help.webex.com/lzr6he/) but you don't remember the link, you can still join it easily from that person's contact card. [Webex App \| Join a Personal Room meeting](https://help.webex.com/ql4cve/) |
| Join from your spaces list          | Join the meeting from the green Join button that appears at the top of your spaces list 5 minutes before the meeting's scheduled start time. See [Webex App \| Join a meeting from your spaces list](https://help.webex.com/nq25ei2/)                        |
| Join from a video system            | Dial the video address, tap the Join button, or automatically join from your video system. See [Join a meeting from a video system](https://help.webex.com/alr3bi/)                                                                                          |
| Join from the action button         | Join the meeting from the action button that appears in the Webex App for iPhone, iPad, or Android. See [Webex App \| Join a meeting using the action button](https://help.webex.com/nvt0evcb/)                                                              |
| Join using the meeting link         | If someone invites you to a Webex meeting, you may receive a meeting link in an email or directly from the meeting host. See [Join a Webex meeting using the meeting link](https://help.webex.com/ga1mli/)                                                   |
