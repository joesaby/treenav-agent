# Change your speaker and microphone settings

Before you begin

- Ensure you allow access to the microphone for calls from the browser, when you sign into the Desktop application.
- If you use Desktop on the Firefox browser, check the **Remember for all microphones**check box when you allow access to the microphone.
- Connect external microphones to your system.

| 1 | Click User Profile at the top-right corner of the Desktop.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| - | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 2 | In the User Settings section, click Speaker and Microphone.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| 3 | Configure the speaker and microphone settings: From the Speaker drop-down list, choose the type of speaker. Click ![Speaker icon.](https://cisco-api.ingeniuxondemand.com/DITA/content/en/us/td/i/500001-600000/510001-520000/510001-511000/510944.jpg) to mute the speaker. To unmute, click ![Speaker off icon.](https://cisco-api.ingeniuxondemand.com/DITA/content/en/us/td/i/500001-600000/510001-520000/510001-511000/510974.jpg). Click Test to test the volume level. From the Microphone drop-down list, choose the type of microphone. Click Test to test the volume level. Changes to the speaker and microphone settings are applicable from the next call. |
