[Manual](#Cisco%5FConcept.dita%5F8a9a0abd-8602-4790-9e2a-8104c8b7ce57)

If Webex App doesn’t connect to a device in your organization automatically, you can manually connect to the device. Just search for the device in the app and then enter the PIN that appears on the device and you’re connected.

To manually connect to a device outside your organization, connect to it by using a 9-character code and then entering the PIN. The PIN just verifies that you’re connecting to the right device because you have to be close to the device to see the PIN on the screen. 

- [Desktop](#id%5F81505)
- [Mobile](#id%5F111221)
- [Web](#id%5F91524)

| 1 | In the app header, click ![Connect to a device icon](https://cisco-api.ingeniuxondemand.com/DITA/content/en/us/td/i/400001-500000/430001-440000/438001-439000/438752.jpg) Connect to a device to view a list of nearby devices and click Connect to select the device you want to use from the list. Click  Search room or device name to search for a device in your organization by name using either ultrasound or Wi-Fi. Use Wi-Fi is only available if your administrator has set it up for your organization. Wi-Fi network information is used to present a list of devices that are in range. The app can list up to 5 nearby devices, even when ultrasound connections aren't working. For more information, see Use Wi-Fi network to discover nearby devices in _Automatic_. To connect to a device outside your organization, click Connect using 9-character code.                                                         |
| - | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 2 | When the PIN displays on the device, enter it in Webex App. If you're in a meeting room with several people, you all use the same PIN to connect your app to the device. Enter the PIN that displays on the device. You can’t use the host PIN for meetings. To see the 9-character code on the device, tap Share \> Webex Share on the device's home screen. Enter the code in Webex App. The PIN remains valid for 5 minutes after it first displays. So, if you make a successful connection, you won't be asked for the PIN in any additional attempts within that 5-minute period. If you try to connect after 5 minutes, you have to enter a new PIN. After you connect manually, if you do nothing on the device for 5 minutes, for example, you don't start a call or meeting, change the device's audio volume, or start or stop video on the device, the connection is automatically disconnected and you need to reconnect. |

If Webex App doesn’t connect to a device automatically, you can manually connect to the device. Just search for the device in the app and then enter the PIN that appears on the device and you’re connected. If Webex App can’t find the device, you can still connect to it by using a 9-character code and then entering the PIN. The PIN just verifies that you’re connecting to the right device because you have to be close to the device to see the PIN on the screen.

[Desk Phone](#Cisco%5FConcept.dita%5Fef4289b1-72d9-4c1e-a1a9-48dc89ef0e8f)

If you're using Webex App to make phone calls, you can set your desk phone to be your default option when making calls from the app. Dial a number from your app, and your call goes through your phone. If you have multiple phone lines, you can [change the active line for calling](https://help.webex.com/en-us/nw31ae5/).

| Select Connect to a Device and select your phone from one of the Personal Devices choices. If you're connected to your desk phone but want to use Webex App instead, click ![Call button](https://cisco-api.ingeniuxondemand.com/DITA/content/en/us/td/i/400001-500000/410001-420000/417001-418000/417356.jpg) and then select My Computer before dialing. |
