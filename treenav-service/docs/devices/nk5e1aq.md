# Lock your meetings on Board, Desk, and Room Series

As a host or co-host you can lock and unlock Webex meetings. When a meeting is locked, you can let people in from the participant list.

1. When you’re having a meeting, tap the screen on your device to get the call controls. Tap  More  and then select  Lock meeting.
2. Your meeting is now locked. To let people in tap the participants list  in the call controls and select Let in. To unlock your meeting tap  More  and then select  Unlock meeting.
