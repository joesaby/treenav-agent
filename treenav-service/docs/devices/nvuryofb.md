# Work with a shared line on your phone

You can share a single phone number with one or more of your coworkers. For example, as an administrative assistant, you might be responsible for screening calls for the person that you support.

When you share a phone number, you can use that phone line just like you would do with any other line.

When working with your shared line, you should be aware of these special characteristics about shared lines:

- The shared phone number can be configured to appear on a maximum of 35 devices.
- The shared phone number appears on all phones that share the number.
- If your coworker is making a call, the shared line on your phone shows the **Remote active** icon  together with the `Shared - Calling` status. Once the shared call is answered, the status changes to `Shared - Active`.
- If your coworker answers a call or is in a conference call, the shared line on your phone shows the **Remote active** icon  together with the `Shared - Active` status. You can add yourself to the ongoing call through the shared line.
- If your coworker puts a call on hold, the shared line on your phone shows the **Remote on hold** icon  together with the `Shared - On hold` status. You can resume the held call through the shared line.
- If your coworker puts a call on private hold, you can see the **Private hold** icon  together with the `Shared - Private` status on the shared line. You can't resume the call from the shared line.
- If you turn on DND on the phone, and meanwhile the shared line receives an incoming call, you can see the **Remote incoming** icon  together with the `Shared - Alerting` status on the shared line.

## Add yourself to a call on a shared line

You or your coworker can join an ongoing call on the shared line to create a three-way conversation.

Before you begin

- Your administrator has configured the Barge feature on the phone.
- A shared line on your phone shows the `Shared - Active` status.

| 1 | On the phone home screen, select or tap the shared line with the status ![](https://cisco-api.ingeniuxondemand.com/DITA/content/en/us/td/i/400001-500000/480001-490000/480001-481000/480973.jpg) Shared - Active. On the phone with single line, the shared-call screen displays directly.On the phone with multiple lines, the calls list displays instead to show all current calls on the phone. You need to select the shared call from the calls list. |
