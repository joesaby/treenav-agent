# Make a conference call on 9800/8875 (Multiplatform)

Conference calling lets you easily add others to an active call on your phone, so you can collaborate instantly without scheduling a meeting. You can add participants from directories, call history, on-hold calls, or add them by dialing their extension numbers.

By default, when the initiator leaves the conference call, the call ends for all participants. However, depending on the call control system configuration, a three-way conference can continue as a two-way call after the initiator leaves.

## Add more people to a call

You can invite additional people to join your ongoing call and hold an ad-hoc conference. 

The maximum number of participants in a conference call varies depending on the call control system and its configuration:

- On phones registered to Webex Calling, a conference call supports up to 6 participants.
- On phones registered to BroadWorks, a conference call supports up to 15 participants.

## View and manage participants

The participant list is only available on Cisco Video Phone 8875.

During a conference call, you can view the participant list. If you have added someone else to the call, you can see the Remove button on the participant list. You can remove only the participants you added.

If you don't see the Participants option in a conference, consult your administrator.

| 1 | During a conference call, tap More ![More Options button](https://cisco-api.ingeniuxondemand.com/DITA/content/en/us/td/i/400001-500000/460001-470000/466001-467000/466557.png) and choose Participants. The participant list displays. |
