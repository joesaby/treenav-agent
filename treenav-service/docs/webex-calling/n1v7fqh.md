# Get certified as a Webex Calling partner

| 1 | If you're not already a Cisco partner, go to [Become a Cisco partner](https://www.cisco.com/c/en/us/partners/become-a-partner.html) to read the eligibility requirements and complete the registration process.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| - | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 2 | Go to the [Partner Program Enrollment (PPE) site](http://p2p.cloudapps.cisco.com/WWChannels/PPP/home.do?actionType=home), and open the Eligible Programs tab. Find the _Webex Calling Partner_ program, click Enroll. ![Partner enrollment dashboard](https://cisco-api.ingeniuxondemand.com/DITA/content/en/us/td/i/400001-500000/460001-470000/463001-464000/463212.jpg) Review the addendum and accept the Terms and Conditions.                                                                                                                                                                      |
| 3 | Complete the Webex Calling Sales and Administration training and exam. [Webex Calling partner specialization - Sales](https://academy.webex.com/learn/courses/1313/webex-calling-partner-specialization-sales/lessons) and one of the following: [Webex Calling Intermediate Administration Training](https://academy.webex.com/learn/learning-plans/144/webex-calling-intermediate-admin-learning-plan) [Webex Calling Professional Administration Training](https://academy.webex.com/learn/courses/2220/webex-calling-administration-professional-2025) Pass the _Webex Calling Administration_ exam. |

What to do next

You’re now able to resell Webex Calling and create trials for your customers.
