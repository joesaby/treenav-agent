# Webex App | Pick up someone else's phone call

If you're not sure what you've been set up with, check out [Find Out What Calling Service You Have](https://help.webex.com/en-us/nbtgrzx/).

- [Mobile](#Cisco%5FGeneric%5FTopic.dita%5F05013694-9e74-414e-8b39-328d92a63787)

[Webex Calling and Calling Hosted by a Service Provider](#Cisco%5FTask%5Fin%5FList%5FGUI.dita%5Fa31a2d84-3826-413a-b020-fc42252208c9)

Depending on your setup, you may or may not get incoming call notifications when a call comes into a pickup group you belong to.

Before you begin

To display the Call Pickup option in the footer, click Call settings in the footer, select Open call preferences and check Display call pick-up in the footer.

To change your call pickup notifications, see [Manage notifications for incoming calls](https://help.webex.com/8la4z9/)

| 1 | When you're set up as a member of a call pick up group, you can pick up calls as follows: You hear a phone call come into a coworker's desk phone—click Call Pickup in the footer of your app. Then, select Call Pickup to pick up a group call (if you're a member of a call pickup group) If you're using Webex Calling and a member of a call pickup group, you can get a call pickup notification in Webex App that a coworker has an incoming call, the call notification includes the pickup group information. Your administrator can configure the audio and visual for the notification. ![Incoming call pickup notification in Webex App including call pickup information.](https://cisco-api.ingeniuxondemand.com/DITA/content/en/us/td/i/400001-500000/460001-470000/465001-466000/465509.jpg) |
| - | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 2 | When you're set up to monitor other users line status and call park extensions (BLF monitoring), you can pick up calls as follows: You can receive a call pickup notification in Webex App that a coworker has an incoming call. This applies to Webex Calling or calling from your Service Provider. Check the person's status on their profile picture. If they're busy or unavailable, click Pick up to answer their call. If you use the calling dock window, you can monitor your BLF list and if anyone has an incoming call, you can easily pick it up. For more information, see [Manage all your phone calls in one place](https://help.webex.com/article/n3t2z74/).                                                                                                                               |

[Unified CM](#id%5F137095)

Depending on your setup, you may or may not get incoming call notifications when a call comes into a pickup group you belong to.

| 1 | Choose one of these options: You hear a phone call come into a coworker's desk phone but you don't get a notification in Webex App—go to ![Call button](https://cisco-api.ingeniuxondemand.com/DITA/content/en/us/td/i/400001-500000/410001-420000/417001-418000/417356.jpg) and then select Call Pickup at the bottom of your Call History. Then, select the appropriate pickup group (Call Pickup, Other Call Pickup, or Directed Pickup). If you choose Directed Pickup, enter the pickup number and then press Enter. Click Pick up again.You get a call notification in Webex App that a team member has an incoming call—check the person's status on their profile picture. If they're busy or unavailable, click Pick up to answer their call.![Incoming call pickup notification in Webex App including call pickup information.](https://cisco-api.ingeniuxondemand.com/DITA/content/en/us/td/i/400001-500000/460001-470000/465001-466000/465509.jpg) |
| - | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 2 | You can then decide whether you want to Answer or Decline the call. And if the caller is a Webex App user, you can even message them. If someone else answers the call before you, you get a notification that there's no call to pick up.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |

[Unified CM](#id%5F137096)

Depending on your setup, you may or may not get incoming call notifications when a call comes into a pickup group you belong to.

Before you begin

You can turn off notifications by tapping your profile picture, selecting Calls, and then under Call Pickup, turn on Mute notifications. 

| Choose one of these options: You hear a phone call come into a coworker's desk phone but you don't get a notification in Webex App—go to Calls ![Call button](https://cisco-api.ingeniuxondemand.com/DITA/content/en/us/td/i/400001-500000/410001-420000/417001-418000/417356.jpg) and then select the Call Pickup tab at the top of your Call History. Then, select the appropriate pickup group (Pickup, Other Pickup, or Pick up a call at a specific extension). If you choose to pick up a call at a specific extension, enter the extension and then tap the green arrow.![pick up someone else's phone call on the mobile app](https://cisco-api.ingeniuxondemand.com/DITA/content/en/us/td/i/400001-500000/440001-450000/448001-449000/448771.jpg)You get a call notification in Webex App that a team member has an incoming call—check the person's status on their profile picture.
