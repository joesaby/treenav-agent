# Webex App | Add a space to your favorites list

- [Desktop](#id%5F81283)
- [iOS](#task%5F4D4E0AE182DECB9619B0B5858CC1E106)
- [Android](#id%5F93923)
- [Web](#Cisco%5FTask.dita%5F253687ca-cdd5-4933-a271-29e54f76e08a)

| Open the space, click ![Star icon for favorite a space](https://cisco-api.ingeniuxondemand.com/DITA/content/en/us/td/i/400001-500000/430001-440000/430001-431000/430844.jpg) beside the space name. You can also right-click on a space in your spaces list and select Add to favorites. If you later want to remove the space from your favorites, click ![Star](https://cisco-api.ingeniuxondemand.com/DITA/content/en/us/td/i/400001-500000/430001-440000/430001-431000/430843.jpg). The space gets added to your favorites list. When you want to find the space again, click ![Filter button](https://cisco-api.ingeniuxondemand.com/DITA/content/en/us/td/i/400001-500000/430001-440000/439001-440000/439461.jpg) to [Filter Your Spaces and Content](https://help.webex.com/iqq278/), and then select Favorites. |
