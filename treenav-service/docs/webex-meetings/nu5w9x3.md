# Webex App | Schedule a meeting from a space

People internal and external to the organization can schedule a meeting associated with a space if they have a Webex host license.

[The number of people who can join a meeting associated with a space](https://help.webex.com/na464seb/) depends on the type of [Webex plan](https://pricing.webex.com/hybrid-work/meetings/) held by the meeting host. 

## End-to-end encryption for Webex App meetings 

Meetings associated with a space support end-to-end encryption. For more information on how to encrypt all your meetings by default, see [Webex App | Set end-to-end encryption for meetings](https://help.webex.com/en-us/article/hgcx2cb/).

[Choose the scheduler](https://help.webex.com/n1c0hwy/) that you want to use to schedule meetings. You can schedule meetings from a space using Microsoft Outlook, Google Calendar, or the Webex App scheduler (if your administrator has [set up your account with Cisco Webex Hybrid Calendar Service](https://help.webex.com/niqovwv/)).

If your administrator hasn't set up your account with Hybrid Calendar or you're using a calendar other than Microsoft Outlook or Google Calendar, use the instructions in _Other Calendars_.
