# Webex App | Set up call routing for when you're away

If you enable Do not disturb (DND) or Call forwarding, they take priority over Away routing.

- [Desktop](#task-template%5F9628da66-045e-4c86-b0c7-1c4755bacfc0)
- [iOS](#task-template%5Ffba711a0-d681-4daf-a7fc-654a9acd3e1e)
- [Android](#task-template%5Fd9ce7529-29ce-42a6-b7de-a1685efbec46)

| 1 | Open Webex and sign in.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| - | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 2 | Click your [profile picture](https://help.webex.com/m67xht/), click Availability and then click Away.                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| 3 | In the Greeting dropdown, choose a greeting message from the available list. Callers hear a recording of the greeting you choose.                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| 4 | In the Ends dropdown, choose from the available options or click Custom to add a custom end date and time.                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| 5 | In the Send calls to dropdown, choose where to send your call: Enter a number, ensure the number is correctly formattedChoose Voicemail onlyChoose a number previously used (we save the last 5 numbers)                                                                                                               |
| 6 | Click Manage notifications and choose if you want to be notified when the caller is greeted.                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| 7 | Click Set. Your availability on your profile picture changes to show Away until your specified time and includes the greeting you chose, unless you already set a custom status. To turn off the setting, click your [profile picture](https://help.webex.com/m67xht/), click Availability \> Away and click Turn off. |
