# Webex App | Mute yourself in a meeting or call

| While you're on a call or in a meeting, select ![Mute](https://cisco-api.ingeniuxondemand.com/DITA/content/en/us/td/i/400001-500000/450001-460000/450001-451000/450675.jpg) at the bottom of the meeting window. You'll know it's working when the button turns red, ![Unmute](https://cisco-api.ingeniuxondemand.com/DITA/content/en/us/td/i/400001-500000/450001-460000/450001-451000/450407.jpg). If you want to unmute yourself, select ![Unmute](https://cisco-api.ingeniuxondemand.com/DITA/content/en/us/td/i/400001-500000/450001-460000/450001-451000/450407.jpg). If you prefer, you can [unmute yourself temporarily ](https://help.webex.com/4bdqv7/). If you see ![Mute, can't unmute yourself](https://cisco-api.ingeniuxondemand.com/DITA/content/en/us/td/i/400001-500000/450001-460000/455001-456000/455603.jpg) beside your name in the participants list, that indicates that the host or cohost has muted you and you can't unmute yourself. If the host or cohost wants you to speak, you'll see a notification to unmute yourself. If you join by phone only, see [Webex App \| Use your phone's keypad to access meeting features](https://help.webex.com/article/nrbe1md) to mute or unmute yourself. If you're connected to a Cisco headset, you can also mute or unmute yourself from the headset. For more information, see [Make and Answer Calls using the Cisco Headset 730 ](https://help.webex.com/n5qci8r/) or [Use Your Cisco Headset 500 Series ](https://help.webex.com/jfhvkf/). |
