# Webex App | Get back to your drafts

- [Desktop](#task%5F6F964BB0986F09E41DC295CE247E708A)
- [iPhone and iPad](#task%5FE03574530265151419A44DECCA762F97)
- [Android](#id%5F129377)
- [Web](#Cisco%5FTask.dita%5Fafcd313a-8096-4f22-85ff-5f28f084e3dc)

| Go to your spaces list, click a space with ![Drafts](https://cisco-api.ingeniuxondemand.com/DITA/content/en/us/td/i/400001-500000/460001-470000/466001-467000/466846.jpg) next to the name, to view the draft message you want to complete. To see all your draft messages, click ![Filter button](https://cisco-api.ingeniuxondemand.com/DITA/content/en/us/td/i/400001-500000/430001-440000/439001-440000/439461.jpg), go to Drafts, then choose the message you want to complete. Also, your message drafts are retained even after a restart, so you can always get back to them. |
