[Webex Meetings and Webex App](#id%5F98276)

### Webex App free version

The free version of Webex App is not available for use in export control restricted countries (Cuba, Iran, North Korea, and Syria) and Crimea, Mainland China, Russia, and Belarus.

The calling services can be deployed but not sold in China, and Macau, branch office only countries and regions.

For example, a partner in the United States can sell and deploy services in the United States. If that same customer also has branch offices in China, and Macau, they can deploy services in China. The customer can't sell to a customer in China, nor can they sell or deploy services in Macau. China, and Macau are not listed in supported countries and regions list.

If deploying a Multi-tenant or Dedicated Instance for Webex Calling, the service can be deployed to any country except Embargo countries.

The following are the embargoed nations with the United States of America: 

- Cuba
- Iran
- North Korea
- Russia
- Syria

For more information, see the [Webex Calling Global Availability and PSTN overview](https://salesconnect.cisco.com/#/content-detail/4704aeff-7a64-4b5f-a6b5-bade158ba6ea).

If you have opportunity and need assistance make sure to contact your Cisco Account Team and Collaboration Sales Specialists.

[Edge](#cisco-webex-edge)

Edge can be sold in any country or region where the following two criteria are met:

- Countries and regions where Cisco supports the sale of Webex Meetings and Webex App paid subscriptions.
- Countries and regions where Cisco supports the delivery Meetings Toll Dial-In Audio and Per Minute committed and uncommitted audio services

For list of countries and regions where audio services are supported please consult the [Webex coverage presentation](https://salesconnect.cisco.com/#/content-detail/094235ba-aa9e-4fa1-aa72-aa21d0311b32) or [Webex audio coverage](https://www.cisco.com/c/dam/en/us/products/collateral/conferencing/webex-meeting-center/cisco%5Fwebex%5Fgpl%5Faudio.pdf) datasheet.

[Important notices about selling Webex Meetings and Webex App ](#id%5F98290)

### Cloud services restricted countries

Cisco is unable to sell Webex Meetings and Webex App paid subscriptions in Mainland China, South Sudan and Yemen at this time. If you have any questions regarding the sale of Webex Meetings and Webex App paid subscriptions in these countries, please contact your local Cisco Account Manager or Partner Account Manager.

### U.S. export control restrictions

Webex Meetings and Webex App are unavailable in Cuba, Iran, North Korea, Sudan, Syria and Crimea due to U.S. export control restrictions.
