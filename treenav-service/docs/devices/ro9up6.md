# Call history on 9800 Series and 8875 phones

The Recents list contains the calls that you made, received, missed on the phone. 

The calls are sorted into the following types: 

- All: Lists all the recent calls.
- Placed: Lists the outgoing calls that you placed.
- Received: Lists the incoming calls that you answered.
- Missed: Lists the missed calls

Calls will not be grouped even if they are to and from the same number. Each call is a single record.

Your phone stores a maximum number of 180 calls in the Recents list. If your Recents list reaches the maximum size, the next new entry overwrites the oldest entry in the list.

| 2 | (For multiline mode) To view the call history for a specific line, choose one of the following actions depending on your phone model. ![For 9811, 9841,9851, and 9861](https://cisco-api.ingeniuxondemand.com/DITA/content/en/us/td/i/400001-500000/480001-490000/489001-490000/489180.png) On the Recents page, press Lines or press the Up navigation key to open the lines list, then select your desired line. ![Icon for 9871 and 8875 for common topics](https://cisco-api.ingeniuxondemand.com/DITA/content/en/us/td/i/400001-500000/480001-490000/485001-486000/485950.png) On the Recents page, select Lines, then select your desired line in the list. |

| 4 | To view the details of a specific call, select the call entry and choose one of the following actions depending on your phone model. ![For 9811, 9841,9851, and 9861](https://cisco-api.ingeniuxondemand.com/DITA/content/en/us/td/i/400001-500000/480001-490000/489001-490000/489180.png) Press Details. ![Icon for 9871 and 8875 for common topics](https://cisco-api.ingeniuxondemand.com/DITA/content/en/us/td/i/400001-500000/480001-490000/485001-486000/485950.png) Tap \> in the call entry to open the details.                                                                                                                                          |

| - | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| 2 | Depending on your phone model, choose one of the following actions to place the call: ![For 9811, 9841,9851, and 9861](https://cisco-api.ingeniuxondemand.com/DITA/content/en/us/td/i/400001-500000/480001-490000/489001-490000/489180.png) Select the call entry and press Call to call the number. ![Icon for 9871 and 8875 for common topics](https://cisco-api.ingeniuxondemand.com/DITA/content/en/us/td/i/400001-500000/480001-490000/485001-486000/485950.png) Tap the call entry to call the number.                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| 3 | (Optional) If you want to modify the dial number before you make a call, do one of the following action: ![For 9811, 9841,9851, and 9861](https://cisco-api.ingeniuxondemand.com/DITA/content/en/us/td/i/400001-500000/480001-490000/489001-490000/489180.png) Press Options \> Edit dial. Edit the number, then press Call. ![Icon for 9871 and 8875 for common topics](https://cisco-api.ingeniuxondemand.com/DITA/content/en/us/td/i/400001-500000/480001-490000/485001-486000/485950.png) Tap \> in the call entry to open the details, then tap Edit dial. ## Add a contact from Recents (Multiplaform)

Adding a contact from the call history helps you save frequently dialed or received numbers quickly, reducing manual input and ensuring easier access for future calls.

This feature is only available on phones registered to Webex Calling or Cisco BroadWorks.

| 1 | Locate the call entry in the Recents list. See [View your recent calls](#task-template%5Fe457f259-c422-4d85-acec-2b55526de681) for the steps.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| - | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| 2 | Do one of the following actions depending on your phone model. ![For 9811, 9841,9851, and 9861](https://cisco-api.ingeniuxondemand.com/DITA/content/en/us/td/i/400001-500000/480001-490000/489001-490000/489180.png) Select the call entry, press Options \> Add to contact. ![Icon for 9871 and 8875 for common topics](https://cisco-api.ingeniuxondemand.com/DITA/content/en/us/td/i/400001-500000/480001-490000/485001-486000/485950.png) Select the call entry to open its details, tap ![The add button](https://cisco-api.ingeniuxondemand.com/DITA/content/en/us/td/i/400001-500000/460001-470000/460001-461000/460436.png). |
| 3 | Edit the contact details and select Save.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
