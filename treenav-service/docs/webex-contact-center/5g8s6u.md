# Authorize service apps for Bring Your Own Virtual Agent (BYOVA)

| 1 | Sign in to [Control Hub](https://admin.webex.com/).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| - | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 2 | Navigate to Management \> Apps, then select the **Service Apps** tab.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| 3 | Choose the required service app. ![To authorize the service app you need to purchase the SKU for the service app.](https://cisco-api.ingeniuxondemand.com/DITA/content/en/us/td/i/400001-500000/480001-490000/489001-490000/489399.jpg) Your organization must have the required subscription for the BYOVA service app before you can authorize it. |
| 4 | Enable the **Authorization** toggle.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| 5 | Check the following fields on the service app: Scopes—Access level definitions for the chosen service app.Data Exchange Schemas—Schema definition for the service app use case.Data Exchange Domains—Authorized domain of the URL for which you need the connection.                                                                                 |
| 6 | Click Save.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |

What to do next

Create the Contact Center AI configuration. For more information, see [Create a Contact Center AI configuration](https://help.webex.com/en-us/article/npbt02j/Create-a-Contact-Center-AI-configuration).
