# Get started with Account Management

[Overview](#Cisco%5FConcept.dita%5F6062ca4a-af57-4882-9e6f-dc85ca698f0b)

This help article applies to subscriptions purchased through [webex.com](http://pricing.webex.com). You can modify what you're paying for and how you're paying for it. 

Enterprise account administrators or partners, see [Get Started with Control Hub](https://help.webex.com/en-US/article/nkhozs6/) for more information on managing your organization, [including billing](https://help.webex.com/n2oivoe/).

**Looking for pricing information?** 

For pricing information and a comparison of features by plan, visit [pricing.webex.com](https://pricing.webex.com/) and scroll down to Compare plan features.

[Manage your account](#concept-template%5F3242f2e3-afec-4b2d-a219-8d31b8fe108e)

On Webex App or your Webex site, click your avatar and select Account Management, or go to <https://web.webex.com/admin>.

To access device management, usage reports, or service setup, go to your menu and select Control Hub.

Accounts with billing addresses in Malaysia, Indonesia, Philippines, Saudi Arabia, and Macau don't have access to Account Management.
