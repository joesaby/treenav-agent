# Check voicemails on 9800/8875 (Unified CM)

- [9811 / 9841 / 9851 / 9861](#task-template%5F911b2d0b-9947-454b-855c-a97136234072)
- [9871](#task-template%5F1ae723a7-6702-4e9a-8ff1-ca0de8dbb9a8)
- [8875](#task-template%5Fbcfed59c-f27f-428f-984b-a9b5039f724e)

When you have new voice messages on your phone, you can see the following indicators on the phone:

- If enabled, the Top 360 LED on Cisco Desk Phone 9841, 9851, and 9861 and the Front Arc LED on Cisco Desk Phone 9811 light up in heartbeating red.  
Your administrator can enable or disable the LED from Cisco Unified CM.
- The voicemail icon  and a message count display.  
For the phone with a single line, the indicators display on the phone home screen.  
For the phone with multiple lines, the indicators display next to the line.

To access your voice messages, do the following:

The steps might vary depending on the features that your administrator has configured on the phone.

Before you begin

Your administrator has configured the voicemail account for you and set up your phone to access the voicemail system.

| 1 | Press Favorite ![the favorite key](https://cisco-api.ingeniuxondemand.com/DITA/content/en/us/td/i/400001-500000/470001-480000/478001-479000/478217.png). You can also press the Messages softkey to access the voice messages if your administrator enables the softkey on your phone. Single line: If only voicemail is configured on the phone, your phone dials the voicemail number directly. In this situation, skip to Step 3. Multiple lines: If only voicemail is configured on the phone, you will see a list in which the message count represents the total number of messages for the related line. In this situation, select a line to dial the voicemail number, and skip to Step 3. You can also press the line key on which the voicemail icon and message count are displayed, and then press Favorite ![the favorite key](https://cisco-api.ingeniuxondemand.com/DITA/content/en/us/td/i/400001-500000/470001-480000/478001-479000/478217.png) to access the line's voice messages. |

When you have new voice messages on your phone, you can see the following indicators on the phone:

- The Top 360 LED on the phone lights up in heartbeating red.  
Your administrator can enable or disable the LEDs from Cisco Unified CM.
- For the phone with multiple lines, the voicemail icon  and a message count display next to the line that has voice messages.  
The voicemail icon  and message count don't display on the phone with a single line. You can check if any digit appears on the Recents .
- A digit appears on the Recents  when your phone has new missed calls, voice messages, or both. The digit represents the total number of new missed calls and voice messages on the phone (including all lines). To view voicemails from call history, see [_8875_](https://help.webex.com/article/nw4k2iz#task-template%5Fbcfed59c-f27f-428f-984b-a9b5039f724e) for details.

When you have unread voicemails on your phone, you can see the following indicators on the phone:

- The strip LED on the phone handset lights up if your administrator has configured the handset LED indicator.
- A digit appears on the Recents  button . It appears also when there are new missed calls on the phone.
- In the Recents list, the voicemail icon  appears. The digit on the icons show the total number of the messages.

To access your voice messages, you can either press Messages  or use the touchscreen by following theses steps:

Before you begin

Your administrator has configured a voicemail account for you and set up your phone to access a voicemail system.

| 1 | On the phone home screen, tap Recents ![recents button](https://cisco-api.ingeniuxondemand.com/DITA/content/en/us/td/i/400001-500000/460001-470000/467001-468000/467999.png) to open the Recents list. The digit on Recents ![recents button](https://cisco-api.ingeniuxondemand.com/DITA/content/en/us/td/i/400001-500000/460001-470000/467001-468000/467999.png) shows the total number of new missed calls and voice messages. The icon next to the current line shows that the phone (including all lines) has new missed calls ![Missing calls icon at the header of the Recents screen (9871)](https://cisco-api.ingeniuxondemand.com/DITA/content/en/us/td/i/400001-500000/480001-490000/483001-484000/483780.png) or voice messages ![Voicemial icon at the header of the Recents screen on 9871](https://cisco-api.ingeniuxondemand.com/DITA/content/en/us/td/i/400001-500000/480001-490000/483001-484000/483781.png), or both ![Combined icons of missing calls and voicemail displayed at the header of the Recents screen on 9871](https://cisco-api.ingeniuxondemand.com/DITA/content/en/us/td/i/400001-500000/480001-490000/483001-484000/483782.png). |
