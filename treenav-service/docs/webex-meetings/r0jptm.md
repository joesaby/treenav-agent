# Review and manage meeting transcripts created by Webex Assistant

- Webex Assistant is available in Meetings and Webex Webinars (available for hosts only in webinars in webcast view).
- In addition to Meetings and Webinars, Webex Assistant is also available for the Webex App. Meetings started or joined from a space don't support Webex Assistant.
- Webex for Government doesn't support Webex Assistant.
- This feature requires Cisco Webex video platform version 2.0 with joining meetings from video systems enabled. [Find your Webex Meetings version number](https://help.webex.com/qgij81/).
- Hosts can turn on or turn off Webex Assistant during the meeting or webinar. You can [automatically turn on Webex Assistant](https://help.webex.com/4pj83q/) whenever you start a meeting
- Closed captions aren’t [turned on by default](https://help.webex.com/lzi8h2/) for the hosts or participants when Webex Assistant is turned on by the hosts. Webex remembers if you used automated captions or not in your previous meeting or webinar. Closed captions automatically turn on or off for your next one, depending on your last usage.
- Webex site admins can turn off automatic postmeeting emails to prevent cluttering inboxes. To turn these postmeeting emails on or off, [contact your Webex site admin](https://help.webex.com/en-us/article/7ksvf1/Contact-your-Webex-site-administrator).
- You can generate the following types of transcripts:

  - A transcript embedded with the video recording. This transcript supports English transcription only.
  - A transcript that's available on the postmeeting site page for the duration that recording is on AND Webex Assistant or closed captions is on. This transcript and any Webex Assistant highlights support transcription in all spoken languages (see [Show real-time translation and transcription in meetings and webinars](https://help.webex.com/nqzpeei/) to see which languages are available to you).
    - To access this transcript, sign in your Webex account, click Calendar, then select Review.

Anyone who has edit privileges for the transcript can download it, play an audio clip, edit the transcript text, and create highlights from the transcript text.

| 2 | Select your meeting. The label, Transcript, appears in meetings that have a transcript. If you don't see the meeting in the list, the host hasn't shared the meeting content with you.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| 3 | Select Review on the Meeting content page.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| 4 | If you're the meeting or webinar host or the host gave you edit privileges for the meeting or webinar content, you can do the following: Play an audio clip—Hover over the text that you want to hear and select Play Audio ![Play Audio](https://cisco-api.ingeniuxondemand.com/DITA/content/en/us/td/i/400001-500000/440001-450000/446001-447000/446491.jpg).Edit the transcript text—Hover over the text that you want to edit and select Edit transcript ![Edit transcript](https://cisco-api.ingeniuxondemand.com/DITA/content/en/us/td/i/400001-500000/440001-450000/446001-447000/446489.jpg). Make your changes and select Save Edit Transcript ![Save Edit Transcript](https://cisco-api.ingeniuxondemand.com/DITA/content/en/us/td/i/400001-500000/440001-450000/446001-447000/446490.jpg).Create a highlight from some transcript text—Hover over the text and select Add to highlights. The text gets added to the Highlights tab as a Notehighlight.Download the transcript—Under the Transcript tab, select the filename of the format that you want to download. Download the transcript in WebVTT (.vtt) or text (.txt) format. If the host gave you view-only privileges, you can play audio clips of the transcript. |

Anyone who has edit privileges for the transcript can play an audio clip and edit the transcript text.

| 1 | From the home screen, select Recordings, then tap Past. The label, Transcript, appears in meetings that have a transcript. If you don't see the meeting in the list, the host hasn't shared the meeting content with you.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| - | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 2 | Select your meeting, then tap Transcript on the Meeting Info page.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| 3 | If you're the meeting or webinar host or if the host shared the meeting or webinar content with you, you can do the following: Play an audio clip—Tap the text that you want to hear and tap ![Play Audio](https://cisco-api.ingeniuxondemand.com/DITA/content/en/us/td/i/400001-500000/440001-450000/446001-447000/446491.jpg).Edit the transcript text—Slide the text left, then tap ![Edit transcript](https://cisco-api.ingeniuxondemand.com/DITA/content/en/us/td/i/400001-500000/440001-450000/443001-444000/443641.jpg) next to the text that you want to edit. Make your changes and select ![Save Edit Transcript](https://cisco-api.ingeniuxondemand.com/DITA/content/en/us/td/i/400001-500000/440001-450000/446001-447000/446490.jpg).Create a highlight—Tap the text, then tap + to add the text as a highlight. Tap \- to remove the text as a highlight. If the host gave you view only privileges, you can still play audio clips of the transcript. |
