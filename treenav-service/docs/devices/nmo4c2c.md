# Access voice messages in a voicemail group inbox

Before you begin

You must have the voicemail group phone number or extension. If you don’t have it, check with your administrator.

| 1 | Perform one of the following actions: From any phone, dial the voice portal phone number. If you don't know your location’s voice portal phone number, ask your administrator. From a Cisco IP Phone, press the Messages key.                                                                |
| - | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 2 | Perform one of the following actions when prompted: If you have both personal and shared voicemail boxes, then enter **\*** and then the voicemail group phone number or extension. If you have only the shared voicemail box, then enter just the voicemail group phone number or extension |
| 3 | Enter the passcode for the voicemail group.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| 4 | Follow the voice prompts.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
