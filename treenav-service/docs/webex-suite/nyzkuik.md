# Webex App | Cisco AI Assistant in meetings

If you can't see the AI Assistant meetings summary feature, contact your organization administrator.

This feature is only available on the Webex Suite meeting platform. To check if you're on this platform and to read about key changes, and forthcoming features, see [more about the Webex Suite meeting platform](https://help.webex.com/en-us/m61d8eb).

If the meeting options are set to the default—Only the host and cohosts can start and stop the summary, participants will need to request that the host or cohosts start the summary to use the feature.

You can also set AI Assistant to start automatically when you click record during a meeting, or to auto-enable for your scheduled meetings. See [Enable Cisco AI Assistant in meetings](https://help.webex.com/zpszvdb/).

Before a meeting can be recorded, summarized, or transcribed, everyone participating needs to give their consent. This way, you’re fully informed and in control of your personal data.

For more information, see [Explicit consent in meetings](https://help.webex.com/article/a1hui6/).

If the meeting host or cohosts select Anyone can start and stop the summary in the meeting options, you can click Stop summary in the AI Assistant panel, to end the meeting summary. If you start, stop, and restart the meeting summary during a meeting, AI Assistant will not save the segment of the meeting that occurred while the summary was stopped, in the post-meeting summary or transcript.                                                                                          |

## AI Assistant settings

Meeting hosts and cohosts can adjust AI Assistant meeting options, to decide who can start or stop meeting summaries, and also decide whether to save summaries and transcripts after the meeting.

| 1 | In the meeting, go to the AI Assistant panel, and click AI Assistant settings ![Settings button represented by a cog icon](https://cisco-api.ingeniuxondemand.com/DITA/content/en/us/td/i/400001-500000/420001-430000/427001-428000/427579.jpg). In the Meeting options window, under AI Assistant, select from: Start and stop summary—choose who can start and stop meeting summaries, select an option in the drop-down list. Only the host and cohosts can start and stop the summary—default selected option. Anyone can start and stop the summary—choose to allow anyone in the meeting to start or stop the summary. Content availability after the meeting—choose to save summaries and transcripts after the meeting. Select an option in the drop-down list. Save summary and transcript—default option. Save summary only Don't save summary and transcript |
| - | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 2 | When you've made your selection, click Apply. Meeting summaries and transcripts are saved to the meeting host's account in Webex App. If your meeting is associated with a [space](https://help.webex.com/en-us/nu5w9x3/) in the app, the links to the summary and transcript automatically appear in that space, where the meeting took place. If you can't save content after the meeting, contact your administrator.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |

## AI Assistant language support

AI Assistant [ supports multiple languages](https://help.webex.com/nhjwwfb/) for in-meeting and post meeting summaries and transcripts, for more inclusive and engaging meetings.

There’s support for 15 spoken languages without requiring a Real-time Translation license. But if you want access to the 120+ caption languages, you'll need to have a [real-time translation license](https://help.webex.com/nqzpeei/).

For customers with a free Webex account, there's support for five spoken languages by default.

| - | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 2 | In the pop-up window, select a spoken language in the drop-down list, then click Set. If you didn't set the spoken language at the start of the meeting, or you want to change the spoken language during a meeting, click ![More Options button](https://cisco-api.ingeniuxondemand.com/DITA/content/en/us/td/i/400001-500000/450001-460000/458001-459000/458469.jpg) More options in your meeting controls. Then, click ![Settings button represented by a cog icon](https://cisco-api.ingeniuxondemand.com/DITA/content/en/us/td/i/400001-500000/430001-440000/438001-439000/438264.jpg) Meeting options, select AI Assistant \> Spoken language for transcript, select a language in the drop-down list, and click Apply. AI Assistant uses this language when generating the post meeting summary and transcript. Webex App uses the [language settings](https://help.webex.com/n739fbo/) that you selected on your device, or browser. AI Assistant provides the in-meeting summary, and responds to your questions in that language, based on your app settings. For example, if a meeting is conducted in Spanish, but your app language is set to French, AI Assistant provides the in-meeting summary, and responds to your questions in French. |

## Request AI Assistant meeting summaries

AI Assistant meeting summaries are off by default. Go to the meeting controls and click AI Assistant .

If you can't access the feature, the host may have made permission changes in the meeting options. You receive a notification that only the host or cohost can enable AI Assistant.

Participants can then ask the host or cohost to enable the meeting summary. When AI Assistant is enabled, you receive a notification that the meeting summary has started.

## Intelligent notifications

When the meeting summary is active, AI Assistant sends notifications asking if you want a summary, when you're late, answer a call, or you return from [Stepping away](https://help.webex.com/ncm18ty/) during a meeting.

### Meeting attendees

- If you join a meeting late, 12 minutes after the start time, AI Assistant sends a notification asking if you want a summary of the meeting so far.
- When you return to a meeting, after setting your status to [Stepped away](https://help.webex.com/ncm18ty/) for at least one minute, AI Assistant asks if you want a summary of what you missed.
- If you answer a call during a meeting, missing more than one minute of the meeting, AI Assistant asks if you want a summary of what you missed.

[Limitations and Further Resources](#reference-template%5Ff0b0ba10-0af0-46c1-aaef-daec1b652407)

### AI Assistant meeting summary limitations

- When AI Assistant is available, Webex Assistant for meetings, which provides highlights and voice commands, becomes unavailable.
- Currently, Ask me anything about the meeting relates to questions about the meeting context only.
- A new summary will only be prompted if there’s sufficient meeting duration to add more information.
- If you select multiple summaries using all the available options in the AI Assistant panel, there may be some summary overlaps, and differences in summary durations.
