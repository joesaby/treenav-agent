# Use help desk on your phone

You can use the help desk feature to quickly dial a number preconfigured by your administrator. 

Before you begin

Your administrator has enabled help desk and configured quick numbers on your phone.

| 1 | (Optional) Select a line.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| - | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 2 | Press Favorite ![the favorite key](https://cisco-api.ingeniuxondemand.com/DITA/content/en/us/td/i/400001-500000/470001-480000/478001-479000/478217.png). The procedure might vary depending on the features your administrator has configured on the phone, do one of the following actions accordingly: If only help desk is configured on the phone, you will see a list of quick numbers. ![For 9811, 9841,9851, and 9861](https://cisco-api.ingeniuxondemand.com/DITA/content/en/us/td/i/400001-500000/480001-490000/489001-490000/489180.png) Select a number and press Call to dial out the quick number.![for 9871](https://cisco-api.ingeniuxondemand.com/DITA/content/en/us/td/i/400001-500000/480001-490000/483001-484000/483152.png) Tap a number to directly dial out the quick number.If help desk and other features (for example, voicemail) are configured on the phone, you will enter the Favorites screen. In this case, select Help Desk first, and then select a quick number to make a call. By default, the help desk menu name is **Help Desk**. It can be customized by your administrator. |
