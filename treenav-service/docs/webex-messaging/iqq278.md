# Webex App | Filter your spaces and content

- [Desktop](#task%5FDD598D505EEADBC18F378BF417C2B1D6)
- [iPhone and iPad](#task%5F1EEDE3C0314ED2452CD65CB41118D1ED)
- [Android](#id%5F129388)

| Click ![Filter button](https://cisco-api.ingeniuxondemand.com/DITA/content/en/us/td/i/400001-500000/430001-440000/439001-440000/439461.jpg) to view all the available filters. Then, select a filter to show just the messages or spaces you want to see. If you select the Unread or Notifications filters, you can click ![Refresh icon](https://cisco-api.ingeniuxondemand.com/DITA/content/en/us/td/i/400001-500000/410001-420000/417001-418000/417388.jpg) to refresh your view and clear out messages after you've read them. To return to your Spaces list, or to get out of a specific filter, click ![Filter button](https://cisco-api.ingeniuxondemand.com/DITA/content/en/us/td/i/400001-500000/430001-440000/439001-440000/439461.jpg) and select Show full list. Your upcoming and ongoing meetings are not visible at the top of the spaces list, when message filters, such as @ Mentions or Flags are selected. When you have one of the following filters selected: Notifications ![Notifications](https://cisco-api.ingeniuxondemand.com/DITA/content/en/us/td/i/400001-500000/470001-480000/472001-473000/472324.jpg)Hidden ![Hidden icon](https://cisco-api.ingeniuxondemand.com/DITA/content/en/us/td/i/400001-500000/430001-440000/439001-440000/439340.jpg)Unread ![Unread](https://cisco-api.ingeniuxondemand.com/DITA/content/en/us/td/i/400001-500000/470001-480000/472001-473000/472325.jpg)Drafts ![Drafts](https://cisco-api.ingeniuxondemand.com/DITA/content/en/us/td/i/400001-500000/460001-470000/466001-467000/466846.jpg) Click the icon to return to the filter list, or click ![Clear filter](https://cisco-api.ingeniuxondemand.com/DITA/content/en/us/td/i/400001-500000/470001-480000/472001-473000/472327.jpg) to clear the filter and return to your spaces list. When you select the Unread ![Unread](https://cisco-api.ingeniuxondemand.com/DITA/content/en/us/td/i/400001-500000/470001-480000/472001-473000/472325.jpg) filter, you can click Mark All as Read to clear the list. If you're on a **Windows** device, click Appearance in the filters list to select your preferences: Show sections—switch between showing sections, or see the latest messages in your space list. Separate DMs and Spaces—add tabs to the spaces list, to organize how you view your spaces. Show compact view—switch to compact view in the spaces list, to see more spaces at a glance. Show recommended messages—show an additional space on top of your list of spaces, which prioritizes messages that you may have missed. See [Recommended messages](https://help.webex.com/6x82v0/) for more information. Appearance preferences, are already visible in the filters list for **Mac**devices. |
