# Record a meeting or webinar

There are two ways to record meetings and webinar. Your [account type](https://www.webex.com/pricing/index.html) and Webex site configuration determine which recording method you can use.

## Play, share, and edit recordings

After you record a meeting or webinar, find your recording so you can edit it, view and edit transcripts, and share it with others. The following list includes things you can do with recorded meetings as the host, cohost, or a viewer:

- [Find your recording](https://help.webex.com/nuwqxdab/)—Access your recordings from User Hub so you can share, download, and edit them.
- [Play a recording](https://help.webex.com/pzh5sw/)—View or download recordings.
- [Share a recording](https://help.webex.com/n462og5/)—Send recordings to people who couldn't attend or who want to review what happened during the meeting or webinar.
- [Create recording transcripts](https://help.webex.com/n18892z/)—Make it easier for people to find specific parts of a meeting or webinar.
- [View, search, and edit recording transcripts](https://help.webex.com/7l12j7/)—If you created transcripts, view them in the recording playback, search for specific text, or edit them to correct errors.
- [Add chapters to recordings](https://help.webex.com/npvlvit/)—Organize recordings into chapters so viewers can easily find parts of the meeting or webinar they want to view.
- [Delete or restore recordings](https://help.webex.com/ncfz8gcb/)—Delete one or more recordings, remove recordings from the trash, or delete them permanently.

Frequently asked questions

[Why can't I record?](#id%5F134751)

- You may not be the host of the meeting. If you were invited to the meeting, ask the host to record.  
If the host isn't in the meeting, a [cohost](https://help.webex.com/en-US/article/n1bqkbv/Add-Cohosts-for-Webex-Meetings-and-Webex-Events-%28New%29) assumes the host role and can record. Once the original host joins, they take back the host role and the ability to record.
- Your site may be out of storage space. [Download](https://help.webex.com/n9r2sit/) recordings to back them up, then [delete](https://help.webex.com/ncfz8gcb/) them in User Hub to make space.
- Recording may not be enabled. [Contact your Webex administrator](https://help.webex.com/7ksvf1/).
- For free users, recording is only available from the desktop app. You can't record if you start a meeting from the web app using a Chromebook.
- If [visual watermarks are added to shared content in a meeting](https://help.webex.com/saxcjg/), your organization may prevent recording the meeting.

[Can I save a recording in MP4 format?](#id%5F134754)

Recordings that you save either to the cloud or to your computer save in the MP4 format. 

Panels, such as the chat panel and participants panel, aren't included in the recording if you record to your computer. Any video files you share using Share File aren't included.

[Where can I find my recording?](#id%5F134753)

Webex stores all your recorded meetings and webinars either locally on your computer or on User Hub. You can [find your recordings](https://help.webex.com/nuwqxdab/) and [share](https://help.webex.com/n462og5/) or [download](https://help.webex.com/aplwwj/) them from User Hub.

If you were invited to a meeting as an attendee or cohost, ask the host of your meeting to get the recording.

[How long does it take to get a recording?](#id%5F134752)

Depending on file size and bandwidth, it can take up to 24 hours to receive your recording. 

For meetings or webinars that last many hours, it's a good idea to make multiple recordings for more manageable file size and easier viewing.

When the recording is ready, we'll send you an email with the recording and all the details you need to play and share it with others.

[How can I tell if my meeting was recorded?](#Cisco%5FTask%5Fin%5FList%5FGUI.dita%5F04b86cd3-d556-4ed1-a3fa-c65e7e96782c)

If you clicked Record  during your meeting, the recording indicator  appears on the top-right of your window, letting you know that the meeting is being recorded. After the meeting, you can also check your meetings list on User Hub to see if you recorded the meeting successfully. Recordings can take up to 24 hours to appear on User Hub, depending on the file size and bandwidth.

| 2 | Select Calendar \> Completed. Meetings that you recorded successfully have a Recording label. |

[How do I play a recording?](#id%5F134756)

Webex recordings in the cloud are stored in the MP4 format, and play on most computers without having to install any new software.

[Ask the host](https://help.webex.com/WBX71898/) to share the recording link and password.

If you're the host, you can play or [download the recording](https://help.webex.com/aplwwj/) from User Hub.

[Can I automatically record a meeting when it starts?](#reference-template%5F5644f2a6-347f-4b62-b414-27ed2241b8f9)

In Webex App, when you [schedule a meeting from the meetings calendar](https://help.webex.com/wy517z/) or [schedule a meeting from a space](https://help.webex.com/nu5w9x3/), you can choose to automatically start recording when the meeting starts.

[Can I convert a recording to MP4?](#id%5F134755)

Recordings save to the MP4 format. If you have recordings in the previously used Advanced Recording Format (ARF), [convert the recordings](https://help.webex.com/p62zmq/) to the MPEG-4 format (.MP4) using a Windows computer.

You can't convert recordings on Mac.

[How do I get a recording transcript?](#id%5F134758)

Site Administrators can enable the automatic transcription of audio for MP4 meeting recordings before you record your meeting or webinar. They can turn on the functionality for everyone, for specific users, or allow users to turn it on for themselves. Check out [Recording Transcripts](https://help.webex.com/uq5009/) for more details. 

Recording transcripts are not available in the Webex free plan.

## Known issues and limitations

- If you start a meeting or webinar by phone, you can't record it.
- Recording on your computer is currently only available from the desktop app. You can't record if you start a meeting from a device using the web app, such as a Chromebook.
- If you [show your camera in front of the presentation](https://help.webex.com/nyg3cqeb/) when you share content, your camera video doesn't appear in the recording.
- If you [sync your stage with everyone](https://help.webex.com/en-us/nic6kmbb/), the first person that you add to the stage appears in meeting or webinar recordings instead of the active speaker.
- Recording playback doesn't fully support Windows Media Player. You can't see Q&A, polling, chat, transcripts, and chapter functionality when viewing the recording.
- You can't choose a recording layout for recordings to your computer.
- In meetings and webinars, content shared using the Share filefeature doesn't appear in recordings saved to your computer. Conversely, on the [Webex Suite meeting platform](https://help.webex.com/m61d8eb/), shared content is included in recordings saved to your computer.
- Recordings to your computer only include audio when you choose Computer audio. If you choose Call me or Call in, the audio doesn't record.
- Recordings to your computer don't capture transcriptions, chat records, participant lists, or files shared during the meeting.
