# Webex App | Multiple lines

To figure out which calling service you've been set up with, see [Find out what calling service you have](https://help.webex.com/article/nbtgrzx/).

[Unified CM—Desktop and Mobile](#reference-template%5F8bfba07f-5d51-4c90-a4df-9c6b6a91fbd0)

### Configuration

You can have one primary and up to 10 shared/other lines, these lines are configured by your administrator.

### Active line

You can [change the active line that you use for calling](https://help.webex.com/article/nw31ae5/). You can switch lines if you need to make outgoing calls from different lines, such as a front desk line, support team line, or an individual line with a different caller ID.

### Answer incoming call

You receive incoming call notifications for the multiple registered lines, the call notification clearly shows which line the call is for. On Windows, you can [manage all your calls in one place with the multi call window](https://help.webex.com/article/n3t2z74/).

### Call history

Your call history includes calls to multiple lines. You can filter the list for specific lines, go to , click filter , and choose a line.

### During a call

When you answer a call, you have the following:

- Only one simultaneous call on each line, you can put one call on hold and answer a call on another line.
- [Hold](https://help.webex.com/article/npvltok/) and resume is available on all lines.
- [Call transfer](https://help.webex.com/article/5er4f3/) is available on all lines. However, you can't transfer a call on one line to a person on another line.
- [Create a conference call](https://help.webex.com/article/nx5rj7s/) is available.
- [Call recording](https://help.webex.com/article/nuu13a1/) is available on each line.
- [Call park or group call park](https://help.webex.com/article/s23ts9/) are only available on your primary line.
- [Call pick up or group call pick up](https://help.webex.com/article/za6jle/) are only available on your primary line.
- Hunt group calls are available on each line.
- CTI control for the desk phone is available on each line. This is only available on desktop.
- Chat and screen sharing are available on each line.

### Call Forwarding

Call forwarding can be set on each line.

### Voicemail

Visual voicemail is only available on your primary line. To access voicemail on your other lines call into the voice portal. You can choose one of your other lines when you call voicemail.

### Configuration

You can have one primary and up to nine shared lines, these lines are configured by your administrator.

For Webex Calling, your administrator can also configure dedicated virtual lines for you.

For Webex Calling, the same shared lines and virtual lines are available on your desktop and mobile.

### Active line

You can [change the active line that you use for calling](https://help.webex.com/article/nw31ae5/). You can switch lines if you need to make outgoing calls from different lines, such as a front desk line, support team line, or an individual line with a different caller ID.

### Answer an incoming call

You receive incoming call notifications for the multiple registered lines, the call notification clearly shows which line the call is for. On Windows, you can [manage all your calls in one place with the multi call window](https://help.webex.com/article/n3t2z74/).

### Call history

Your call history includes calls to multiple lines. You can filter the list for specific lines, go to , click filter  and choose a line.

For calls on a shared line, made or received by another user, you can see the name of the user.

### During a call

When you answer a call you have the following:

- Only one simultaneous call on each line, you can put one call on hold and answer a call on another line.
- [Hold](https://help.webex.com/article/npvltok/) and resume is available on all lines. You can also put a call on hold and somebody else can resume that same call.
- [Call transfer](https://help.webex.com/article/5er4f3/) is available on all lines. However, you can't transfer a call on one line to a person on another line.
- [Create a conference call](https://help.webex.com/article/nx5rj7s/) is available when both calls are on the same line.
- [Call recording](https://help.webex.com/article/nuu13a1/) is only available on your primary line
- [Call park or group call park](https://help.webex.com/article/s23ts9/) are only available on your primary line
- [Call pick up or group call pick up](https://help.webex.com/article/za6jle/) are only available on your primary line
- Call pull is available from your deskphone only on your primary line
- Chat and screen sharing are only available on your primary line.

### Call Forwarding

Call forwarding can be set on each dedicated virtual line, but not shared lines.

### Voicemail

Visual voicemail is only available on your primary line. 

To access voicemail on your other lines (non-primary lines), access the voicemail portal by selecting the desired line from the Call voicemail at the top right corner of the voicemail icon. 

Webex Calling only: Voicemail transcription is only available for your primary line.
