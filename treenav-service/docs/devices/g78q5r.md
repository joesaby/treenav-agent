# Make a conference call on 9800/8875 (Unified CM)

You can converse with several people in a single call. When you’re on a phone call, you can dial another person and add them into the conversation without having to schedule a meeting. As a host, you can remove a participant from the conference call. 

According to your administrator's configuration, other participants (non-host users) may also have the removal permission.

If you have multiple phone lines, you can join two calls across two lines. The conference will end automatically when all participants hang up.

## Add more people to a call

On an active call, you can initiate a conference call or add additional people to a call by using the Conference key. Depending on the phone model, the Conference key can be a hard key or softkey. The Conference hard key  is available on 8875 and 9800 Series phones (except 9811). The softkey appears as Conf on 9811 and Conferenceon other models.

According to the configuration on the server side, a conference call can accommodate up to 64 participants (including yourself).

## View and manage participants

You can view the participants who have joined the current conference call. If you are the initiator of the conference call, you can remove participants from the conference call. According to the administrator's configuration, all participants may have the permission to remove participants.

If you don't see the Participants option in a conference, consult your administrator.

| 1 | During a conference call, locate and select Participants (n), where <n> represents the count of people who have joined the conference. The count displays only on Cisco Desk Phone 9861 and 9871, and Cisco Video Phone 8875. The participants list displays. |

| 2 | To remove a participant, select Remove on the participant. The conference call ends automatically when all participants hang up.                                                                                                                              |
