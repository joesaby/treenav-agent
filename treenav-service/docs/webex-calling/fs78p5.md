# Assign organization account roles in Control Hub

## Managing roles in Control Hub

We recommend that you share administrative responsibilities within your organization. Even in small organizations, we recommend having more than one person holding a full administrator role to ensure availability.

As a full administrator, you can assign various administrative roles to people in your organization, to streamline responsibilities and share accountability for managing the organization.

For example, a compliance officer can access user-generated content as necessary for legal/compliance purposes. This is likely to be a different person than a support administrator, who can access analytics and troubleshooting information.

This article describes how to manage administrative roles and lists the privileges associated with each role. Carefully plan out which roles your organization needs and how you want to divide responsibilities.

If you want to create a customized administrator role, see [Customized roles in Control Hub](https://help.webex.com/x7l1qbb/).

If you're managing multiple Webex organizations for your customers, see [administrator roles in Partner Hub](https://help.webex.com/16gmxfb/).

### Assign roles to users

| 1 | Sign in to [Control Hub](https://admin.webex.com/), go to Management \> Users, and choose a user. If you're a partner, open the customer organization where you want to assign roles, then access that organization's Users page.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| - | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| 2 | In the Summary tab, go to Administrator roles. If the user has roles assigned to them, they're listed here.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| 3 | Click **\>**, then select roles to assign to the user. For meetings site administrator roles, click Edit next to Webex Site administrator roles. Select roles for each Webex site that you want the user to manage. If you change the _Organizational_ role of a user who has an existing _Services_ role, you could affect their existing services roles. Review the user's services roles if you change their organizational role. The location administrator role is exclusive from other types of admin roles. You can't assign a location administrator role to a user who is already assigned other admin roles. To assign locations to the user, check Location admin and [add the locations](https://help.webex.com/ajh6iy/). If a user is assigned as user and device administrators or device administrators, they can't administer Webex Calling. |
| 4 | Click Save. It may take up to 10 minutes for users to gain access to their new roles.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |

Want to see how it's done? Watch this [video demonstration](https://youtu.be/yLfyiL9FFYk) on how to assign organization account roles in Control Hub.

## Role details

Each role determines the privilege level you have in Control Hub. If you have full administrator privileges, you can assign one or more roles to any user in your organization.

When you set up your account for the first time, the administrator from the partner organization becomes the full administrator for your organization, and can set up additional administrators. 

Users that you add to your organization have no administrative privileges at first. Here's a list of the prebuilt roles in Control Hub and why you might want to assign them to a user:

- Full administrator—Full administrators have access to every aspect of how an organization is run. They can add and remove users, distribute Webex licenses, create and modify company policy templates, and change organization settings.
- Read-only administrator—Read-only administrators can view and monitor all areas of Control Hub, but don't have permission to make any organizational changes.
- Support administrator—Support administrators are intended to provide logistical support to full administrators. They only have access to organization analytics and reports, in addition to troubleshooting tools. They may also view organization support cases.
- User and device administrator—User and device administrators have access to the essential day-to-day workings of an organization. They can add, manage users, and set up and manage devices.
- Device administrator—Device administrators focus solely on device management. This includes [registering or deregistering new devices and phone numbers](https://help.webex.com/article/n9r1aac/), [generating activation codes](https://help.webex.com/article/ndb7w0db), and [managing workspaces](https://help.webex.com/article/1mqb9cb).
- Compliance officer—Compliance officers moderate and oversee user-generated content. They can access the [Events API](https://help.webex.com/article/nmbm0jk/) and [eDiscovery reports](https://help.webex.com/article/nr70c1m) to see user-generated content in Webex App. They can also override the default content retention policy to support legal cases.
- Security activity administrator—Security activity administrators assist full administrators in [monitoring changes](https://help.webex.com/2tc9yx/) to organization settings and troubleshooting Calling and meeting logs.
- Advanced troubleshooting access—The advanced troubleshooting access role allows full administrators, read-only administrators, and support administrators to access the Live Meeting page in the Troubleshooting tab. Full administrators with this permission can also join in-progress meetings to provide troubleshooting assistance.

The Webex Calling CDR API Access role allows the user to have access to the REST API that collects data for Webex Calling Detailed Call History reports. See the [Webex Calling Detailed Call History API documentation](https://developer.webex.com/docs/api/v1/reports-detailed-call-history) for details.

### Advanced Troubleshooting Access Role

This role allows full administrators, read-only administrators, and support administrators to access the Live Meeting page in the Troubleshooting tab, and lets full administrators join meetings that are in progress with just the click of a button. If participants are having issues during meetings, administrators with this role can quickly find those meetings to join and help sort out the issues.

For more information about the Live Meeting page and the Admin Join feature, see [Advanced Diagnostics and Troubleshooting in Control Hub](https://help.webex.com/ni3wlvw/).

Full administrators can assign the Advanced Troubleshooting Access role to another full administrator, read-only administrator, or support administrator within their organization. Full administrators can’t assign the Advanced Troubleshooting Access role to themselves. Another full administrator must assign the role to them.

### Webex Site Administrator roles

You can only assign Webex site administrator roles to users in Control Hub-managed meeting sites. These roles can't be assigned in Control Hub to manage Site Administration-managed meeting sites.

Webex site administrators only have access to the users and settings for the Webex site that they're assigned to in Control Hub. You can assign a user as an administrator for more than one Webex site. Users that are assigned with the site administrator role must sign in to Control Hub once after being assigned the role before they can get admin access to the meeting sites.

Full administrators have Webex site administrator and Meeting content administrator privileges, by default. These roles cannot be modified or revoked.

 If your organization has a lot of Webex sites to manage, assign users as Webex site, Webex user, or Webex read-only administrators to define ownership and to split up administrative responsibilities for each Webex site.

Webex site administrators and Webex user administrators can only manage Webex Meetings-related settings for users in customer organizations to which they're assigned.

Webex site administrators don't use a Webex Meetings license for the Webex site that they're assigned to, but they won't have any host privileges. If you want a Webex site administrator to have hosting privileges, assign a host license to them.

#### Meeting Content Administrator role

If you want to allow Webex site administrators to also access, retrieve, and manage meeting artifacts, such as recordings and transcriptions, through APIs or a web interface, you can toggle a switch to assign them as a Meeting Content admin. This role allows you to assign users with granular access to specific Webex sites instead of allowing them access to all sites.

To assign the Meeting Content admin role to a user:

1. Sign in to [Control Hub](https://admin.webex.com), go to Users, and choose a user.
2. In the user's Profile tab, find Administrator roles.
3. In the Services section, click Edit next to Webex site administrator roles.
4. Toggle the Assign Meeting content admin for all eligible sites on.  
The user must be assigned one of the Webex site administrator roles for a site in order to have access to the Meeting Content admin role.
5. Click Done.
6. Click Save.

### Webex location administrator

You can distribute administration across the organization by [delegating administration of certain services in locations](https://help.webex.com/760wbi/). This enables you to efficiently handle location-level management without impacting the entire organization.

### Webex Events administrator

The Webex Events administrator role has access to Webex Events (formerly Socio) and can grant access to others.

When the user with this role signs in to Control Hub, they can:

- See the Services \> Events page.
- On that page, click the Launch Webex Events portal button to open Socio.  
(Administrators who do not have this specific role see an inactive button)
