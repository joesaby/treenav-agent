Customers using Android 9 won't be able to upgrade Webex App or do a fresh install of the app on and after November 1, 2023\. #### Meet instantly with a one-time link—Windows, Mac, iPhone, iPad, and Android

Start a meeting immediately with a unique one-time meeting link. Invite other people to join by sending them the meeting link without all the steps needed to schedule a meeting.
