# Change the video layout of a connected device

To get an idea of how the different layouts display the shared video and content on your device, check out [Webex Rooms | Video Stream Layouts](https://help.webex.com/article/zp1dhab/).

- [Desktop](#Cisco%5FTask%5Fin%5FList%5FGUI.dita%5F2cadfb75-e63c-4510-bb62-3d1c490afdda)
- [Mobile](#Cisco%5FTask%5Fin%5FList%5FGUI.dita%5F339d4db2-1f09-469c-81e0-ddd0bb4556c1)

Make sure you're already on a call or in a meeting and that you're [connected to a device from Webex App](https://help.webex.com/article/nf29igm/) or [connected to a device from Meetings](https://help.webex.com/nqx2ohdb/Use-the-Webex-Meetings-desktop-app#id%5F93254).

| 1 | Choose one of the following options: If you're on your device and using Webex App, go to Webex App on your desktop, click Open meeting window or Open call window, and then select Device layout.If you're on Webex App on your desktop, click Device layout.If you're on your device and using Meetings desktop app, go to the app on your desktop, click Return to meeting, and then select Device layout. |
| - | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| 2 | Choose one of the available layouts and see how it looks on your device.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| 3 | Click Device layout and then choose the options that work best for you: Show shared content on device—show shared content on your device instead of your desktop. Show my self-view video on device—show your self-view video on your device instead of your desktop.                                                                                                                                        |

Make sure you're already on a call or in a meeting and that you're [connected to a device](https://help.webex.com/article/nf29igm/).

You can only change the layout of a connected device from Webex App.

| 1 | Choose one of the following options: If you're on your device, go to your mobile app and tap Open meeting window or Open call window. Then, tap ![](https://cisco-api.ingeniuxondemand.com/DITA/content/en/us/td/i/400001-500000/430001-440000/434001-435000/434517.jpg) and select Device layout.If you're on your mobile app, tap Device layout. |
| - | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 2 | Choose one of the available layouts and see how it looks on your device.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| 3 | Tap Device layout and then choose the options that work best for you: Show shared content on device—show shared content on your device instead of your mobile device. Show my self-view video on device—show your self-view video on your device instead of your mobile device.                                                                    |
