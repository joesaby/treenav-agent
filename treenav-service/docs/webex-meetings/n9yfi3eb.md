# Webex App | Share content in a meeting

If you are a host or cohost of the meeting, you can disable sharing for all other participants during a meeting. See [Webex App | Turn off content sharing for participants in a meeting](https://help.webex.com/article/yih0de/).

If you prefer, you can [share a file](https://help.webex.com/nmjspyx/), [share only a portion of your screen](https://help.webex.com/ngexff8/), [share your camera](https://help.webex.com/nvsg6rx/), or [share background music or audio without sharing content](https://help.webex.com/nay9u8w/).
